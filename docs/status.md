## Simulated (generated by our own code; no field data behind it)

- **Every data file in `data/`**, including `showcase.csv`. Showcase is 30
  days from our generator (`dtwin/simulate/generator.py`, seed 42) with eight
  scripted faults (`SCENARIOS["showcase"]` in `dtwin/simulate/faults.py`).
  Replay plays the file; nothing is added at playback.
- **Live mode's default source** (`python -m dtwin.live`): the same physics,
  stepped one sample at a time, with faults injected from the dashboard. The
  analytics receive only the samples; they are never told what was injected.
- **All detection figures** (held-out 11/12, 4 % self-test limit, latencies):
  measured against simulator ground truth. Real probes are probably noisier
  than the 0.25 K the simulator adds.
- The teammate's blind test files: received as-is; their WTI and Status
  columns are synthetic (formula-generated) and are never cited as validation.

## Needs real test data or hardware validation before field use

1. **Detection thresholds on a real healthy stretch.** Slope alarm 1.06 has a
   thin margin (healthy max 1.058 in simulation). Re-measure the healthy p95
   and the residual sd on CPRI's data before trusting alarm rates.
2. **The nameplate of the actual unit.** A wrong kVA reads as a thermal fault
   (results.md). Rating, rises and losses from its test certificate.
3. **Probe placement and noise.** The oil probe must sit in a top-oil pocket;
   the ambient probe shaded. Their noise sets every "sd" figure.
4. **OC / SC test results** to replace the illustrative circuit (the OC/SC
   reduction is implemented and waits for numbers).
5. **The 3D component states that rest on inference** (LV connection
   resistance from V-I regression, core from voltage only) need a thermal
   image or a joint-resistance measurement to confirm once.
6. **The live link with real hardware.** The contract is fixed
   (`docs/live-contract.md`); the ESP32 path has not been run end to end.
7. **Protection behaviour above 140 C.** We assume protection trips there;
   the real relay settings decide.
8. **Overlap and missed-step limits** (results.md): a sensor fault during an
   asset fault needs an independent second probe.
